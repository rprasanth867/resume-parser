## 🎤 Presentation Structure

### Recommended Flow (20 minutes):

**Introduction (2 minutes)**
- Introduce yourself and team
- Project name and tagline
- Brief overview

**Problem (3 minutes)**
- Current recruitment challenges
- Statistics showing the problem
- Why it matters

**Solution (8 minutes)**
- Your application overview
- Architecture diagram
- 5 core features (1.5 min each)
- Demo or screenshots

**Technical Details (3 minutes)**
- Tech stack
- AI integration
- Scoring algorithm

**Impact (2 minutes)**
- Results and metrics
- User feedback
- Future roadmap

**Q&A (2 minutes)**
- Questions from audience
- Contact information

## 💡 Presentation Tips

### Do's:
✅ Practice beforehand (at least 3 times)
✅ Know your numbers by heart
✅ Prepare backup screenshots if demo fails
✅ Speak slowly and clearly
✅ Make eye contact with audience
✅ Show enthusiasm for your project
✅ Explain technical terms simply
✅ Have a timer visible

### Don'ts:
❌ Don't read directly from slides
❌ Don't put too much text on slides
❌ Don't skip the problem statement
❌ Don't go over time limit
❌ Don't apologize for your work
❌ Don't use technical jargon without explaining
❌ Don't face away from audience
